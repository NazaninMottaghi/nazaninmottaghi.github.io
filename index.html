About

Where mathematics meets digital markets

I'm a Researcher in Digital Finance at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, blockchain, DeFi, tokenized real estate and financial econometrics. With a Ph.D. in Mathematics and an interdisciplinary background, I study how emerging digital technologies are reshaping financial systems, investment behavior and real estate markets.

Beyond research, I care deeply about teaching, mentoring and interdisciplinary collaboration, and I enjoy projects that connect rigorous quantitative methods with real-world applications in finance and technology.

Looking for my papers? They are published under Fatemeh (Nazanin) Mottaghi, and KTH lists me as Seyedeh Fatemeh Mottaghi.

Outside academia: nature walks, learning languages, and quiet time with my plants in Stockholm.

Blockchain & DeFiMarket infrastructure

How can tokenized assets trade on existing exchanges?

Asks which legal, institutional and technical changes are needed to bring tokenized real-world assets into regulated exchanges. Integration turns out to depend on how ownership is legally recorded, how settlement, custody, finality and recovery duties are shared between institutions, and how trading and post-trade systems exchange consistent information. The analysis ends in a concrete change map for a Nordic hybrid model.

Funder
Nasdaq Nordic Foundation
Period
Jan 2026 – Dec 2027
Role
ResearcherProject led by Bertram Steininger, with Shufan Ma (KTH and DTU)
Method
Comparative review of Nordic securities-recording rules, EU DLT Pilot evidence and live DLT market-infrastructure implementations, with Sweden as a focused case
Output
Tokenized Real-World Assets on Existing Exchanges: Legal, Institutional, and Technical Requirements Working paperwith S. Ma, B. Steininger and Z. Yang

Do real estate tokens move with the stock market?

Maps the direction of information flow between real estate tokens and REITs, equities, crypto and gold. Tokens mainly receive information from equity markets, and in turbulent periods such as COVID-19 the link with Ethereum becomes two-way.

Data science & MLEconometrics

Forecasting volatility with GARCH and neural networks

GARCH captures volatility clustering, recurrent networks (GRU, LSTM and a combination) refine the forecast, and a simplified risk parity allocation uses it. The authors report that the resulting strategies outperform standard risk/return portfolios.

Method
GARCH, GRU and LSTM, risk parity allocation
Data
Multi-asset portfolio of US and international equities
Output
Expert Systems with Applications, vol. 229, 2023 Publishedwith L. Di Persio, M. Garbelli and K. Wallbaum
Related role
Associated Researcher, IPM (Mar – Dec 2023), neural networks for financial time series and portfolio optimization

Econometrics

Which ESG markets transmit volatility, and which absorb it?

Maps how volatility spills between ESG equity indices and an oil benchmark. Global and developed-market ESG indices and European carbon-efficient equities are net transmitters, while emerging-market ESG, clean technology and oil are net receivers, and so more exposed to outside shocks.

Method
Granger causality, impulse responses and the Diebold–Yilmaz spillover index
Data
Daily returns, 2014–2022, ESG indices of different regions and sectors plus an oil benchmark
Output
Frontiers in Sustainability, vol. 6, art. 1612279, 2025 Publishedwith M.E. De Giuli, L. Di Persio and A. Tanda
